## Task briefing (paraphrased; spoiler-free)

A collage contains four detailed close-up photographs of a large nativity-scene diorama. Investigate the display shown in the photographs. According to an official source, **what was the minimum number of individual pieces used in its 2023 installation?**

Submit the count as a plain integer, without punctuation or units, on the [original challenge page](https://en.osint4fun.eu/advent2024/20241202123000/).

The full image is necessary to solve the task. Open the [official image](https://en.osint4fun.eu/img/advent2024/671e6493358d7.jpg), or download it using `bash data/fetch.sh`.

## Objective

Identify the correct installation and establish the officially reported minimum number of pieces for its 2023 version. Keep a record of the authoritative source and its publication context. Submit the number to the official answer form.

## Prerequisites

- Basic web searching and source evaluation.
- Comparing distinctive visual details across photographs.
- Distinguishing a primary/official count from estimates or secondary reports.
- Keeping source URLs and a reproducible research trail.

## Hints (open only if needed)

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Start by describing distinctive objects and structures visible in the collage. Search using combinations of those features and the event type rather than generic image labels.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Treat the year 2023 as an important filter. Once you identify a plausible installation, compare its visual characteristics with the supplied collage and check whether its size or composition varies between years.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 3</summary>

Prioritize statements published by the installation's organizers or the institution responsible for the display. Confirm that the figure describes the number of **pieces**, not the number of figures, visitors, or square meters.

</details>
